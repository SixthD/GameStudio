Game Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Game Studio is an independent Android application that helps users create and play HTML5 games using AI services. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information when you use it.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, Game Studio may handle the following information:

- AI API keys: Users provide their own API keys to access supported AI providers.
- Prompts and game content: Text prompts, source code, and other content submitted to AI services to generate or improve games.
- Local projects: Games, project files, settings, and related data saved within the application.
- Support communications: Information you choose to provide when contacting us for assistance.
- Account and community information: If account or community features are enabled, information required to provide those features may be processed by the relevant service.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in game prompts or other content unless it is necessary.

2. AI Providers and Third-Party Services

Game Studio can connect to supported AI providers using API keys supplied by users.

When you request AI-powered features, relevant prompts and content may be sent to the AI provider you select. Those providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

Users are responsible for reviewing the applicable provider terms, privacy practices, and API pricing before using these services.

Game Studio is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the AI providers it supports.

3. API Keys and Security

Game Studio is designed to store API keys locally using the application's available secure storage mechanisms.

No storage method can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their devices and API credentials, avoid sharing API keys, and revoke compromised keys through the relevant provider.

4. Local Storage and Exported Projects

Game Studio can save projects and settings on your device and allows you to export game files.

Locally stored projects may remain on your device until you delete them or remove the application. Exported files may be stored or shared wherever you choose to save them.

Game Studio does not automatically make locally saved projects public merely because they exist in the application.

5. Accounts and Community Features

If online profile, authentication, or community publishing features are available, the information necessary to operate those features may be processed by the services used to provide them.

Published content may be visible to other users according to the relevant feature's settings and terms.

This policy should be updated if additional information is collected or new online features change how data is handled.

6. Data Sharing

Game Studio does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be transmitted to selected AI providers when you use AI features, to service providers needed for enabled online features, or when disclosure is required by applicable law.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored projects and settings can be deleted by the user through the available application or device controls.

Information processed by third-party services may remain subject to those services' retention and deletion policies. If you have an account or submit a support request, contact us for assistance with applicable deletion requests.

8. Children's Privacy

Game Studio is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Users should follow the age requirements and terms applicable to the application and the third-party services they use.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Game Studio's features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be published at the same webpage address with a revised update date.
